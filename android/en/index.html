Travel Wallet · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026 · Android

Se Hwan Choi, the operator of Travel Wallet for Android, explains below how the app handles trip records, location features, external services and advertising.

1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the Travel Wallet Android app and this information page. The operator does not provide user accounts or a server that synchronizes trip data. Contact: sehwani87@gmail.com.

2. Trip and expense records

Trip names, cities, dates, budgets, currencies and expense amounts, place names, categories, dates, addresses and coordinates you choose to save are used to manage trips, calculate conversions and display statistics. Records are stored in the app’s on-device storage and are not sent to the developer’s servers. Android backups and files you export are described in section 6.

3. Location and place search

If you allow location access, the app uses your location to display your current position and find nearby places. It does not track location in the background. The current position may be kept temporarily in memory; coordinates you save with an expense remain with that record. You can enter trips and expenses manually without granting location permission.

Place searches send the query, language, IP address and, when available, coordinates rounded to three decimal places to Geoapify. Rounded coordinates can still represent a precise location. If your location is unavailable, the selected trip’s city may be included in the query. Unsaved queries are not kept as a permanent search history by the app. Request information processed by a provider follows that provider’s retention policy.

4. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for banners and Google UMP for consent. Google may process IP-derived location, advertising IDs and app-related identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information to deliver and measure ads, analyze usage and prevent abuse.

The app updates UMP consent information and handles any required consent form before initializing the advertising SDK and requesting ads when UMP permits requests. A previously valid consent status may be used if an update fails. Personalization and other ad behavior depend on consent signals and Google’s applicable policies. Where ad privacy options are available in Settings, you can review or change your choices. Android also lets you reset or delete the advertising ID. Deleting that ID does not stop all ad-related processing. Apple ATT is not used on Android.

5. External services

  • Google Maps / Google Play services: maps, map interactions and device location. Google may process IP addresses, device and SDK metadata, a Maps SDK identifier and crash information. Google Privacy Policy
  • Geoapify: place searches. Geoapify Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob / UMP: advertising and consent. Google’s use of information from partner services
  • Frankfurter: rate requests send currency codes and a reference date when needed, without entered amounts, expense records or locations. Connection information such as an IP address may also reach the service. Service information
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this page; it may process visiting IP addresses, browser information and access logs. GitHub Privacy Statement

Providers may process information outside your country. Their processing, retention and rights procedures follow the linked policies and your applicable consent choices.

6. Retention, backup, deletion and export

Trip and expense records remain until you delete them. You can delete individual expenses or trips, or use the travel data deletion option in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes on-device app data.

If Android backup is enabled, trip records, expenses and app preferences may be included in Google account backups or device-to-device transfers and restored later. Backup retention and deletion depend on device/account settings and provider policies. Deletion inside the app does not immediately remove existing backups or exported files.

CSV export saves a file to the destination you choose. A cloud destination is governed by its provider’s policy. Exported files must be deleted separately. Map caches, search service request logs and advertising information follow the respective providers’ retention policies.

7. Your choices and requests

You can change location permission in device settings and use ad privacy options when offered in the app. Send requests about access, correction or deletion of information the developer controls to the email above. Support emails and the sender’s address are processed through the email service to answer the request. The developer cannot remotely access or delete records stored only on your device.

8. Safeguards and policy changes

The app uses HTTPS for external API communication. Your device lock and backup settings, along with external providers’ safeguards, also affect data protection. This page and its update date will change when relevant features, services or requirements change. Contact the operator with concerns about personal information.
